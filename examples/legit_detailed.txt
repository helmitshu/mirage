Northwind Analytics is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer for our Toronto office. Hybrid, two days a week onsite.

About us
We build metering and billing infrastructure for energy retailers. 120 people, founded 2016, profitable since 2020. Our stack is Python, Postgres, and Kafka on AWS. See https://northwind-analytics.example.com/about for the team and our engineering blog.

The role
You will own our usage ingestion pipeline, which processes about 40 million events a day. Recent projects include cutting pipeline latency from 90 seconds to under 10 and migrating our rating engine to a new pricing model.

Responsibilities
- Design and operate high throughput data pipelines in Python
- Lead incident reviews and drive follow ups to completion
- Mentor two intermediate engineers on the team
- Participate in an on call rotation, one week in six, with compensation

Requirements
- Six plus years of backend engineering, three plus with Python
- Production experience with Kafka or a similar streaming platform
- Comfortable with Postgres query planning and index design
- You communicate clearly in writing; most collaboration is async

Compensation
CAD 150,000 to 175,000 base depending on experience, plus equity and an annual bonus targeting 10 percent. Health and dental from day one, four weeks vacation.

Apply
Send your resume and a short note about a pipeline you have operated to jobs@northwind-analytics.example.com with the subject Senior Backend Engineer. We review every application and respond within ten business days.
